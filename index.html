About

I'm Paul — most people know me as Monsai. I'm a full-stack engineer and web designer based in Baybay City, Philippines, working mainly with React on the frontend and Laravel on the backend.

Most of my recent work has been building systems for local government: tracking documents, monitoring flood risk, running port ticketing, and registering pets city-wide. These aren't demo projects — they're systems real offices depend on every day, which means they have to be dependable, understandable to non-technical staff, and built to hold up under real usage.

Before that, I built authentication tooling and a virtual touring platform, and designed and developed websites for travel and pest-control businesses — so I'm comfortable moving between backend systems work and front-facing design.

I hold a BS in Information Technology from Western Leyte College of Ormoc, where my thesis project was an online business permit system — my first taste of building software for local government, before it became most of my day job.

Civic systems, in production

Four systems built for Baybay City's local government — each one replacing a manual, paper-based process with something residents and staff actually rely on.

01 / SYSTEMS
SYS-01 · DOCS DEPLOYED

Document Tracking System

Gives government staff a single place to log, route, and check the status of official documents as they move between offices — replacing manual logbooks with a searchable, auditable trail.

ReactLaravelMySQL
SYS-02 · PORT-POS DEPLOYED

PPA Baybay District POS

A point-of-sale and ticketing system for the Philippine Ports Authority's Baybay District, tracking vehicles and passengers in and out of port in real time and reconciling total sales automatically.

ReactLaravelTicketing
SYS-03 · BAYBANTAY DEPLOYED

Baybantay

Flood-risk monitoring across Baybay City. Residents submit real-time reports when flooding hits their area, routing directly to the CDRRMO so response teams can act immediately instead of waiting on delayed word-of-mouth.

ReactLaravelGeo / Reporting
SYS-04 · E-PAWS DEPLOYED

e-PAWS

A household-level dog registry for every barangay in Baybay City, helping the city monitor pet population growth, control breeding, and reduce dog-related accidents through better record-keeping.

ReactLaravelRegistry
